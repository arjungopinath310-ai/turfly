Turfly SMS templates

For DLT registration (India) and MSG91. Generated from src/lib/notifications/sms.ts, the same text the app sends. A copy-and-paste version is in docs\sms-templates.txt.

What you register

ItemValue
Sender ID (header)TURFLY (6 letters). If the portal rejects it, use a 6-letter header that matches your business or brand name and tell your developer.
Template typeService Implicit for all four (sign-in codes and booking updates for your own users)
CategoryThe closest match on your portal, e.g. "Tourism and Leisure" or "Others"
Templates4: turfly_login_otp, turfly_booking_confirmed, turfly_match_reminder, turfly_balance_due
Every message is plain English with "Rs." instead of the rupee sign: one special character turns an SMS into a Unicode SMS, which halves its length and doubles the price. Each message fits in one SMS (160 characters).

Step 1 · DLT registration (once)

  1. Pick any one telecom DLT portal (a registration works on every network), for example Jio TrueConnect, Airtel DLT, Vi (Vilpower) or BSNL.
  2. Register as a Principal Entity (your business): PAN, GST certificate or Udyam registration (free online), ID of the authorised person and the letter of authorisation the portal provides. One-time fee of about ₹5,000–6,000; approval usually takes a few working days. Note your Entity ID.
  3. Register the header TURFLY for service messages.
  4. Add the four content templates below: type Service Implicit, header TURFLY, and paste each text exactly. Note each DLT Template ID.
If a reviewer asks you to change the wording, the app's text must change too (it must match word for word): edit src/lib/notifications/sms.ts (or ask your developer), then regenerate this document.

Step 2 · MSG91

  1. Create an account at msg91.com and complete their KYC.
  2. Add your DLT Entity ID and the sender ID TURFLY.
  3. Sign-in code: MSG91 → OTP → Templates → new template. Paste the MSG91 text of template 1 (with ##OTP##), enter its DLT Template ID and sender TURFLY.
  4. Other three: MSG91 → SMS → Templates (Flow) → new template for each. Paste the MSG91 text exactly, with the variables written as ##var1##, ##var2##… in that order, and enter the matching DLT Template ID.
  5. Copy MSG91's template ID of each into .env (names below), and the Auth key (MSG91 → API) into MSG91_AUTH_KEY.
  6. Buy SMS credits (roughly ₹0.20–0.30 per SMS).

Step 3 · Switch it on

  1. Open .env with Notepad, fill in MSG91_AUTH_KEY, MSG91_OTP_TEMPLATE_ID, MSG91_TPL_BOOKING_CONFIRMED, MSG91_TPL_REMINDER, MSG91_TPL_BALANCE_PENDING.
  2. Restart the website: powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts\restart-server.ps1. The Turfly server window warns if a template ID is missing.
  3. Admin → Fees, policy & offers → Reminders: tick sms.
  4. Test: sign in with your own number; the code should arrive by SMS (no longer shown on screen). Make a test booking and check the confirmation SMS.

Cost per booking: up to 4 SMS (confirmation, 24 h and 2 h reminders, balance due), plus 1 per sign-in: about ₹1–1.5 per booking.

The four templates

1 · turfly_login_otp

Sign-in code (sent when someone signs in with their mobile number)

DLT template text (copy exactly)
{#var#} is your Turfly login OTP. It is valid for 5 minutes. Do not share it with anyone. - Turfly
VariableMeaningExample
16-digit code482913
Example SMS (97 characters, 1 SMS)

482913 is your Turfly login OTP. It is valid for 5 minutes. Do not share it with anyone. - Turfly

MSG91 template text (MSG91 > OTP > Templates)
##OTP## is your Turfly login OTP. It is valid for 5 minutes. Do not share it with anyone. - Turfly

MSG91 template ID goes in .env as MSG91_OTP_TEMPLATE_ID

2 · turfly_booking_confirmed

Booking confirmed (sent when the online advance is received)

DLT template text (copy exactly)

VariableMeaningExample

2turf nameKickoff Fives Manjeri

4amount paid online49
5amount due at the turf951
Example SMS (125 characters, 1 SMS)

Booking TF-QZM8TJ confirmed: Kickoff Fives Manjeri, Tue 17 Nov, 8:00-9:00 PM. Paid Rs.49 online, Rs.951 due at turf. - Turfly

MSG91 template text (MSG91 > SMS > Templates (Flow))
Booking ##var1## confirmed: ##var2##, ##var3##. Paid Rs.##var4## online, Rs.##var5## due at turf. - Turfly

MSG91 template ID goes in .env as MSG91_TPL_BOOKING_CONFIRMED
